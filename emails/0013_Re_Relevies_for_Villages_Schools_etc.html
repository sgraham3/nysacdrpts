Good morning everyone,

Thank you all for getting back to me so quickly!

I had a meeting with our County Attorney and discussed the different processes with him.

He agreed with the advice that Trish was given by the Receiver. Basically, "that we are NOT supposed to change the dates to accommodate the calendar," so going forward we will put the last date of the month, "and if it falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or federal holiday, then by law (collectors must) accept the next business day."

He also suggested meeting with all of the village clerks to make sure that we have all of their local law/resolutions information for tax bill purposes up to date and to remind them to keep us informed if they make any changes.

Law reference time! I'll put the paragraph the language is in before the link.

Village taxes: sent to County within 15 days of end of village tax collection (4.(a)

NYS Open Legislation | NYSenate.gov)

School taxes: sent to County not later than November 15th (2.

NYS Open Legislation | NYSenate.gov)

Unpaid water/sewer: Town law 198 covers a lot of topics but Water (3(d)

NYS Open Legislation | NYSenate.gov) specifically says that no later than November 1st the town clerk must file with the Supervisor a list of unpaid water accounts and the supervisor shall then transmit such statements to "the board of supervisors

of the county at its next regular meeting"

Alternatively what I do: just make your life easy and say everything needs to be submitted at the same time the Town budget is submitted to the County. Towns have until November

20th to pass a budget (NYS Open Legislation | NYSenate.gov), the Town Clerk must deliver within 5 day the final budget to the Town Supervisor (NYS

Open Legislation | NYSenate.gov), and the Town Supervisor must send the budget to the County within 10 days of receiving it from the Clerk (NYS Open Legislation | NYSenate.gov).

So - at the end of the day, December 5th is the absolute latest a town can submit their budget to the County and the budget should include the amounts to be raised for any relevies to be added to tax bills.
