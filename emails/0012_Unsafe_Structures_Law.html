Last year, the Jefferson County Legislature passed a new resolution concerning cleanup/demolition of dangerous properties. I reached out to Candy and she graciously passed on Lewis County's process for handling the relevy of the costs involved if the property owner doesn't pay them when billed. However, any ideas/suggestions on the subject would be appreciated!
