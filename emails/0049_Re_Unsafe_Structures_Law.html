Just as an FYI for towns, the town has to pass a local law to allow demolition costs to be added as a charge on the tax bill. Lawn mowing/cleanup costs do not need a local law. We require a

cover sheet for reporting all re-levies to us which I highly recommend because otherwise they can send you a bunch of random bills and will say "relevy these please!" without even a hint of what the total is supposed to be.
