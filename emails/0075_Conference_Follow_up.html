Hello,

Happy First day of the Spring of Deception, or Third Winter...hard to know where we're at!

Wanted to follow up with everyone after the Conference of Awkwardness, well for me anyway, and I assume

for my fellow "early adopters". If you read, Crossing the Chasm (highly recommended), it examines the dynamics faced by innovative tech products. The theory is there is a chasm between early adopters of new tech and the pragmatists who are generally the early

majority. Primarily a sales and marketing book, it explores differing expectations and has some relevance to the PSO roll out.

Anyway, mostly I want to provide an update on the NDA issue. I have asked for and received some clarification.

I do have the latitude to discuss operational functionality of my county's RPSO currently in production. However, I need to be mindful of any ongoing discussions and development scenarios originating from the constituent's group, or any observations, process

suggestions and enhancements, or wish list items that may be on the drawing board but are not currently in the production environment.

It would have been valuable to have this information a couple of days ago. Armed with that, I certainly

would have spent more time in the networking suite!

There were struggles with our early adoption. And yes, our T&C tax cycle was difficult. We had issues

to overcome...one battle after another! Yes, we were late and yes, the phones were busy, but genuine typical complete bills and rolls went out. Sometimes you just don't know there is an issue until you get to that point. I give tremendous credit to my staff,

our town clerks and collectors, our Treasurer's Office, and especially the RPSO team for their effort and dedication to seeing it through. I hope the product benefitted from our pain.

Each county is different. We have our own unique set of challenges and operational structures. I will

say I encourage every county in a position to do so to sign on. With each addition and with each new obstacle overcome the system improves. There really isn't another alternative, for us at least. V4 is literally a dead language. Your next hardware upgrade

could render it incompatible and good luck finding someone to bang out a patch with a script to fix it.

Feel free to reach out, I'll respond if I am able.
