I prepare the omitted tax letters/calculations for the assessors. I give the letters to them when they give me their files for tentative roll in April.

They check them over, sign, and mail them out in May (at least 10 days before grievance day).

We batch-load them on the tax bills. We do NOT use R/S 9.

We started doing them about six years ago. We never did them before that. I only started it because we were getting into the assessing business. By law, the

assessor is the only one that does them and they HAVE to (read "shall" in the law). Well, we know how that goes sometimes. I wanted to do them county-wide or not at all since it's not really fair for only some towns to do them.

It goes on the tax bill, not a chargeback.
