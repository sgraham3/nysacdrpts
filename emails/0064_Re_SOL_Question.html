You will be notified from the State. You will go to the SOL section and your list for the town will be there

with the approved values and what years were approved. You will provide this to the Finance Department (or whoever bills for SOL in

your county) and they will issue a bill for the years and value approved. Then you can move to R/S3. This can take years

before it gets approved.
