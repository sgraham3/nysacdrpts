485-b Question

From: "Wiley, Paul" <00000010df4a36f7-dmarc-request@LIST-NYSAC.US>

Date: 2026-03-31 16:44

Someone asked for the County's resolution for our 485-b exemption and in digging around I have found a couple of resolutions from the 1970s but nothing more recent. The most recent one from 1979 seems to be adopting a special 485 exemption

for "eligible business facilities" that meet certain criteria, but we just found that one and I do not know of any property that has received that. We have been applying a basic, ten year 485-b exemption for decades around here. The property owner has to apply

for it, and the exemption starts at 50%, goes down 5% per year for ten years. Improvement value has to be $10,000 or higher, with no maximum amount.

My question for you: do we need a local law or resolution for the basic 485-b, or is that an exemption that applies unless you opt out? I would not like to find out that we have been doing something wrong for the past 25 or 30 years...
