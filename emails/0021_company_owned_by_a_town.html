company owned by a town?

From: Kathleen Kearney <KathleenKearney@ONGOV.NET>

Date: 2026-03-11 15:31

We have a situation where a town sold their town garage property in December 2024 to a not-for-profit company who rents the land to an adjacent shed company for display space. No exemptions were filed, so the property was put back onto the tax rolls.

NOW, after not paying school taxes either, the town filed a correction stating that the town-owned property exemption should not have been removed, because the property is still town-owned. Well, sort of. Apparently (though the assessor at the time was not

aware) this not-for-profit company is solely owned by the town as a redevelopment initiative. The certificate of incorporation states that it must be totally separate from the town. This is not considered an "IDA enumerated in the general municipal law" (per

RPTL §412-a), it's just a company that the town created, probably to protect their budgets/finances from the risk/reward of property investments in the town. We asked them for more information from the assessor, so they filed an exemption under 420-b on 2/17

and expected that to cover them for 2026 county/town taxes.

It's only $1650, but still...what would y'all do? It's not my call to determine exemption eligibility for this roll year, but for a correction almost a year after when they should have applied for their exemption just because they "didn't know" they'd lose

their existing exemption?

Thanks y'all!
