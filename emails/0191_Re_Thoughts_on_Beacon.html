So, we have Beacon and we have changed A LOT to work for us, as we have stand alone assessors, and I have to add a lot of notes so that the public can

be regularly updated with what is happening without files (we get databases only 4X/year). We have re-ordered things, have requested some sections stay open, while others be collapsed, changed functions of adding sales, and the map and layers blow away what

we had with our former basic e-Parcel map setup. They have now just added the 5217 button to the bottom of the property when you open it.

With Beacon, we put the most relevant sections to us up towards the top, we've added highlighting to notes, and we like that you just have to scroll down

to get to uploads, property card, tax map, and parcel map. It's a one stop shop, whereas with IMO, there was a lot of clicking around to find things.

We're pretty happy. T
